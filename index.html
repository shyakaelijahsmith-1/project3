All About Sports

Explore different types of sports and their benefits

Football Basketball Athletics

Football

Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. It is played between two teams of 11 players using a ball.

Basketball

Basketball is played by two teams of 5 players. The goal is to score points by shooting the ball through the hoop.

Athletics

Athletics includes running, jumping, and throwing events such as sprinting, long jump, and javelin.

Benefits of Sports

  • Improves physical fitness
  • Builds teamwork skills
  • Boosts mental health
  • Encourages discipline
